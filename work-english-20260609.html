Word of the Day
alignment /əˈlaɪn.mənt/
n. 对齐；一致；达成共识
In a work context, "alignment" means that everyone on a team shares the same understanding of goals, priorities, or decisions. It's one of the most common words in async communication — used when you want to confirm that people are on the same page before moving forward.
Just want to make sure we have alignment on the scope before I start building — can you confirm the v1 cut?
After the async thread, I think we're in alignment on the approach. I'll go ahead and update the spec.
Sentence Patterns
"Just to make sure we're aligned — ___."
确认共识 · Slack / 文档评论 · 会前同步
只是想确认一下我们的理解一致——……（在推进前核对认知）
Just to make sure we're aligned — the goal for this sprint is to ship the onboarding redesign, not the notification system, right?
Just to make sure we're aligned — you're expecting a full write-up by Thursday, not just a rough outline?
"I'll leave a comment here so we have a record of ___."
异步协作 · 文档 / PR / 设计稿
我在这里留个评论，这样我们有……的记录（异步留痕，方便后续追溯）
I'll leave a comment here so we have a record of the decision — we're going with the simplified flow for now and revisiting post-launch.
I'll leave a comment here so we have a record of what was discussed in the call — easier than digging through Slack later.
"No need to jump on a call — here's the context: ___."
异步优先文化 · Slack 消息 · 减少会议
不用开会——背景是这样的：……（主动提供上下文，避免不必要的同步会议）
No need to jump on a call — here's the context: the API change affects only the mobile client, and the fix is already in review.
No need to jump on a call — here's the context: I've already synced with the design team and they're good with the new layout.
Mini Scenario
Hey team,

Dropping this here async since I know everyone's heads-down today.

Just to make sure we're aligned — the plan is to freeze the feature list for the June release by end of day Thursday. Anything not in the doc by then gets pushed to July.

I'll leave a comment here so we have a record of this decision. If anyone has blockers or wants to flag something for the cut, please reply in thread by Wednesday noon.

No need to jump on a call — here's the context: this is just a scope lock, not a scope cut. Everything on the backlog stays prioritized, we're just being clear about what ships when.

We have good alignment on the big picture — let's keep the momentum going.

Quick Tip
💬
异步沟通的核心是"减少来回"。每次发消息前问自己：对方看完之后还需要问我什么？把那个问题的答案也一起写进去。这叫 front-loading context（提前给足背景），是高效远程协作最重要的习惯之一。
