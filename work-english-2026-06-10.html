Meetings & Calls
Word of the Day
consensus /kənˈsensəs/
n. 共识；一致意见
A general agreement reached by a group after discussion. When a team reaches consensus, everyone may not be thrilled, but they can all commit to moving forward together.
We spent 20 minutes debating the approach, but eventually reached a consensus to go with the simpler solution.
There's no consensus yet on the release date — let's put it on the agenda for tomorrow's sync.
Sentence Patterns
"Can we get to a consensus on ___ by end of this call?"
会议推进 · 决策收尾
我们能在这次会议结束前就……达成共识吗？
Can we get to a consensus on the MVP scope by end of this call?
Can we get to a consensus on who owns this before we wrap up?
"I want to make sure we're aligned on ___ before we move on."
会议对齐 · 避免误解
在继续之前，我想确认大家对……的理解是一致的。
I want to make sure we're aligned on the success metrics before we move on.
I want to make sure we're aligned on the timeline before we move on to resourcing.
"Let's timebox this to ___ minutes and then make a call."
会议效率 · 时间管理
我们把这个话题限时……分钟，然后做决定。
Let's timebox this to five minutes and then make a call on the direction.
We've been going back and forth — let's timebox this to ten minutes and then make a call.
Mini Scenario
The team has been debating two different approaches to the onboarding flow for the past 15 minutes. Sarah finally says: "Let's timebox this to five more minutes and then make a call — we can't keep going in circles." After a quick round of feedback, the group still seems split. Mark steps in: "I want to make sure we're aligned on the core problem we're solving before we pick a solution." That reframe helps — and within a few minutes, the team reaches consensus on the simpler approach, with a note to revisit after the first round of user testing.
Quick Tip
💡
In English meetings, "consensus" doesn't mean everyone is 100% happy — it means everyone can live with the decision and commit to it. A useful phrase to signal this is: "I can live with that." It's honest, collaborative, and moves things forward without pretending there's full enthusiasm. Much more natural than saying "I agree" when you don't fully agree.
